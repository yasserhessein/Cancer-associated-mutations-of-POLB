# Enhancing POLB Mutation Classification with a Random Forest and PSO Hybrid Model
Our latest research paper, titled

"Enhancing POLB Mutation Classification with a Random Forest and PSO Hybrid Model"

has just been published in the Journal of Information Systems Engineering and Management 
DOI: https://doi.org/10.52783/jisem.v10i46s.8772
Volume: 10 | Issue: 46s | e-ISSN: 2468-4376

In this work, we propose a novel hybrid model combining Random Forest with Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) to enhance the classification of POLB gene mutations, which are critical in cancer diagnostics. The model achieved high performance metrics, demonstrating its potential in biomedical data analysis and precision medicine.
